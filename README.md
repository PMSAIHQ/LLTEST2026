# LLTEST2026
Driving Test Mock for Goa State Of India. Contains over 400 questions.

🚦 Goa Road Rules Practice Test
A free, single-page practice test for people preparing for the Learner’s Licence (LL) test or refreshing their knowledge of road signs and traffic rules. It runs entirely in your browser: no sign-up, no installation, no data collected.


Important: This is an independent practice tool. It is not the official RTO test and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Goa Directorate of Transport or any government body. Always refer to official sources for the latest rules and penalties.
What it does.


431 questions on road signs, hand signals, speed limits, penalties and documents, overtaking and parking, and safe driving.


Sign questions both ways: “What does this sign mean?” and “Which sign means Give Way?” (with look-alike signs as options).


30-second timer per question (can be turned off)
Choose how many questions (10, 20, 30, 50, 100 or all).


Categories to focus on one topic, and a Weak spots mode that replays questions you got wrong
Hard mode: 15-second timer, closest look-alike signs, tricky answer options, and −1 for each wrong answer
Results screen with score, accuracy by category, and a review of your mistakes.


Progress history saved on your own device
A “Finish test now” button if you want to stop early
Works on phones and computers, in light and dark mode

How to use
Open the website (or open index.html in any modern browser).
Pick a category, question type and number of questions.
Tap Start test. Answer before the timer runs out.
Review your mistakes at the end and try again.
A score of 70% or more is shown as a pass in this tool. This is only a guide for practice.
Privacy
No accounts, no tracking, no ads.
Your scores and weak-spot list are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Clearing your browser data removes them.
Nothing is sent to any server.
Accuracy and updates
The questions come from a learner’s-licence question bank. Some answer keys were reviewed and corrected where they were clearly outdated (for example, penalty amounts).
Speed limits and some penalties differ by state and change over time. Goa’s current rules may differ from what the question bank says. Please verify with the Goa Directorate of Transport / official RTO sources.
